PhD student in Industrial Engineering at Pontificia Universidad Católica de
Valparaíso (PUCV), member of the Laboratorio de Sistemas de Transporte
Inteligente (LSTI). Background in mechatronics engineering. My research
focuses on Digital Twin implementation in port logistics, designing the data
capture and fusion layer that feeds an interpretable-by-design AI model for
coordinating landside truck flow at smart gates.

See the [Research/Projects](/projects/) page for current lines of work, and the
[CV](/cv/) page for education and employment history.

## Research Interests

- Digital Twin
- Port logistics
- Interpretable AI
- IoT
- Sensing
- Data Science
