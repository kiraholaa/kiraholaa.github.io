## Project title

Development of low-cost medical training simulators for practical
training and clinical instruction in basic cardiopulmonary
resuscitation (BLS) procedures.

## Status

Currently in execution (development phase).

## General objective

To develop low-cost adult, child, and infant basic life support (BLS)
cardiopulmonary resuscitation simulators with real-time objective
feedback, at a production cost under USD 150 per unit, achieving at
least a 20% increase in effective compressions within standard ranges
among BLS users.

## Specific objectives

1. Organize the project by defining scope, schedule, and costs,
   producing an approved detailed work plan, and ensuring that over
   the 28-week development period, deviations between planned and
   executed costs and timelines do not exceed 10%.
2. Design the mechanical, electronic, and software structure of the
   simulators, meeting at least 95% of user-defined requirements, with
   each feature directly traceable to basic life support (BLS)
   criteria.
3. Build functional simulator prototypes with a materials cost not
   exceeding USD 150, meeting defined accuracy and response-time
   levels, with a minimum continuous-use autonomy of 4 hours and
   support for at least 10,000 compressions.
4. Validate the technical performance and educational value of the
   simulators through a pilot study and a quasi-experimental study,
   demonstrating that they meet the previously established performance
   criteria.
5. Document the simulators' development and results by producing
   technical manuals, an instructor's guide, and submitting one
   scientific manuscript.

## Field of application

Medical simulation applied to health-sciences and first-aid education,
focused on developing low-cost medical simulators for teaching and
training in basic cardiopulmonary resuscitation (BLS). Aimed at the
general public, with priority given to university students in health
and technology fields (medicine and engineering) and professionals in
continuing education, to strengthen the practical skills needed to act
immediately and effectively in academic, community, and hospital
settings.
